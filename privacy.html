Your photos. Your choice.

Privacy Policy · Effective October 1, 2026. Kithframe is operated by Mark Tripoli. Contact: me@marktripoli.com.

Local first

Family names, reference photographs, drafts, and downloaded portraits are stored on your device. Browsing, adding profiles, and reviewing saved portraits do not require an account or upload. Your operating system or browser may back up device data according to your settings. Deleting the app or clearing browser storage may permanently remove local photographs.

What leaves your device

Only after you confirm a portrait or retouch do we send the selected source photos and instructions to our processing service. Reference metadata is stripped before upload. Fixed-scene processing uses a background-removal model on our server. Custom scene prompts go to OpenAI without sending your source people. Retouches send the source portrait, mask, and instructions to OpenAI. Review each request and do not include information you do not want to send.

Accounts and payments

Supabase processes email authentication, private image storage, credit balances, and job records. Railway hosts the processing service. RevenueCat verifies Apple and Google in-app purchases. We keep your email, account identifier, transaction identifiers, credit entries, and session status to provide the service and prevent duplicate billing. Card details stay with the app store.

Retention and providers

Our service removes source uploads after a session finishes or fails. Temporary completed portraits expire within 24 hours; abandoned uploads are cleaned up within 24 hours. Account-linked payment and job records are removed on account deletion, except where legal obligations apply. We retain unlinked store transaction identifiers and credit-grant state to prevent an already used receipt from granting credits again; these records no longer contain your account identifier or email. Provider backups and OpenAI’s abuse-monitoring retention are separate from our live storage cleanup; OpenAI API data can normally be retained up to 30 days. We do not train a custom model on your family, sell photographs, run advertising trackers, or use facial recognition.

Interrupted account deletion

We retain the deleted account’s random identifier, a hash of its private deletion-recovery key, and confirmation status so deletion can safely finish after an interrupted connection. This recovery record contains no email, photographs, or usable sign-in credentials. RevenueCat and the app stores may retain their own payment records under their policies and legal obligations.

Consent, children, and pets

This service is for adults aged 18 and older. You must have permission from every person shown, including permission from a parent or guardian before including a child. Only upload photographs you own or are authorized to use. Do not submit private, intimate, exploitative, or unlawful content. We do not knowingly offer accounts to children.

Your controls

Remove a family member or portrait from this device in the app. Exported copies in your photo library are yours to remove separately. Delete your signed-in account in Account to remove live account records, server files, and this device’s Kithframe photos; unused credits are forfeited. Contact us to request access or correction. We cannot recover photographs deleted from your device.

Security and changes

Transfers use HTTPS in production. Server photos are private and accessible through short-lived authenticated links. API and payment secrets never belong in the app. No storage system is risk-free. We update this policy when our processing changes and will seek any consent the law requires. Questions or concerns: me@marktripoli.com.
