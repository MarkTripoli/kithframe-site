Your family. Your direction.

Choose a setting, decide how your family belongs in the picture, and review the result. Original-photo placement is the default; AI-generated poses are optional.

1. Add your family

Choose up to 3 clear photos of each person or pet. The first photo supplies the original pose and the identity reference for optional AI posing. Tap another reference photo to make it first. Use one subject per source photo, with their entire head visible and no filters. Similar lighting and camera angles produce more natural group portraits.

2. Choose a scene

Choose from studio, outdoor, autumn, Halloween, winter, Christmas, spring, and summer backdrops. Fixed scenes cost 2 credits with original-photo placement and reuse the same backdrop. Custom backgrounds cost 5 credits and use AI to make a setting from your description. Background direction is separate from family arrangement.

3. Arrange your family

For every fixed or custom scene, describe where each person or pet belongs using their names: left, right, centre, together, or in front. Original-photo placement moves and scales the source cutouts without redrawing faces, clothing, or poses. A request that needs a new pose cannot be fulfilled in this mode; choose AI posing explicitly or supply a source photo showing the pose.

4. Optional AI posing

To request interactions such as holding a baby or seating children together, explicitly choose AI posing and describe the pose. This costs 5 credits with either a fixed or custom background. OpenAI receives the selected source photographs and direction to generate the new pose. It can change faces, bodies, clothing, or pet markings: exact likeness is not guaranteed. No automated likeness score replaces your review.

5. Review and refine

Check every person, pet, pose, and placement before saving or sharing. A retouch costs 1 credit: tap up to 4 areas and describe each requested change. The AI editor receives an exact image mask, and we enforce that mask when assembling the result. Everything outside the marked areas stays unchanged. A face edit deliberately changes appearance and must be reviewed again.

6. Save something worth keeping

Portraits download to this device. Approved portraits can be saved as PNG files or shared. Local photos remain available offline. Kithframe is not a cloud backup: export important photographs. If you close the app while a portrait is being created, return within 24 hours to download it.

Credits and failed sessions

Only verified app-store purchases add credits. Your signed-in account holds the balance, so use the same email on another device. Purchased credits do not expire. A session reserves credits once; technical failure returns them once. Changing your mind or disliking a completed portrait does not automatically refund a session. Contact support for a genuine problem.
