Made from your real photos.

Kithframe brings your original people and pets into a new scene. It does not automatically invent replacement faces.

1. Add your family

Choose up to 3 clear photos of each person or pet. The first photo supplies their pose. Tap another reference photo to make it first. Use one subject per source photo, with their entire head visible and no filters. Similar lighting and camera angles produce more natural group portraits.

2. Choose a scene

Fixed scenes cost 2 credits and always use the same backdrop. Custom scenes cost 5 credits and use AI to make a background from your description. People keep their source clothing and pose. A background prompt cannot create a new outfit or body pose.

3. Keep your people, change the setting

AI separates your people and pets from their original backgrounds. We use their actual photograph pixels, resized and arranged together. Faces and fur markings are not redrawn. Cutout edges, partial bodies, proportions, and mismatched lighting still need your review.

4. Review and refine

Check every person and pet before saving or sharing. A retouch costs 1 credit: tap up to 4 areas and describe each requested change. The AI editor receives an exact image mask, and we enforce that mask when assembling the result. Everything outside the marked areas stays unchanged. A face edit deliberately changes appearance and must be reviewed again.

5. Save something worth keeping

Portraits download to this device. Approved portraits can be saved as PNG files or shared. Local photos remain available offline. Kithframe is not a cloud backup: export important photographs. If you close the app while a portrait is being created, return within 24 hours to download it.

Credits and failed sessions

Only verified app-store purchases add credits. Your signed-in account holds the balance, so use the same email on another device. Purchased credits do not expire. A session reserves credits once; technical failure returns them once. Changing your mind or disliking a completed portrait does not automatically refund a session. Contact support for a genuine problem.
