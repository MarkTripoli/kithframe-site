Your photos. Your direction.

Add photos, choose a background or draw freely, and describe the portrait. No reusable family profiles, names to register, or face models.

1. Add photos for this portrait

Upload 1–100 photos in one or several batches. Group photos, pets, and multiple photos of the same person are welcome. Photos receive numbers, not identities. They stay with this draft and its portrait history on your device; a new portrait starts empty. Choose clear, well-lit photographs. Large batches are sent as numbered contact sheets, which can reduce small-face detail.

2. Pick or draw a background

Choose a classic or seasonal scene, describe your own setting, or draw a freehand scene or composition guide. You can change these choices in any order. A fixed-scene portrait costs 2 credits; a custom or sketched-scene portrait costs 5. The selected scene artwork or rendered drawing guides the generated result, rather than being a pixel-for-pixel promise.

3. Write one prompt

Describe who should appear, where they belong, how they interact, and the mood or style. Refer to photo numbers when helpful, especially when the same person appears in several images or a group photo contains people you do not want included. For a custom setting, include the background in the same prompt. There is no prescribed wording or minimum character count; a non-empty prompt is required, up to 2,000 characters.

4. Review the AI request

Every new portrait is AI-generated. After your permission, OpenAI receives all selected photo references, the chosen scene or rendered sketch when applicable, and your prompt. Faces, bodies, clothing, poses, and pet markings can change; exact likeness is not guaranteed. No automated likeness score replaces your review. The app does not create a reusable family identity or train a face model from your photos.

5. Review and refine

Check every person, pet, pose, and placement before saving or sharing. A retouch costs 1 credit: tap up to 4 areas and describe each requested change. The AI editor receives an exact image mask, and we enforce that mask when assembling the result. Everything outside the marked areas stays unchanged. A face edit deliberately changes appearance and must be reviewed again.

6. Save something worth keeping

Portraits download to this device. Approved portraits can be saved as PNG files or shared. Local photos remain available offline. Kithframe is not a cloud backup: export important photographs. If you close the app while a portrait is being created, return within 24 hours to download it.

Credits and failed sessions

Only verified app-store purchases add credits. Your signed-in account holds the balance, so use the same email on another device. Purchased credits do not expire. A session reserves credits once; technical failure returns them once. Changing your mind or disliking a completed portrait does not automatically refund a session. Contact support for a genuine problem.
