Your photos. Your direction.

Everyone belongs
in the picture.

Upload photos, choose a classic or seasonal background—or draw a scene freehand—and write one prompt. Group photos, pets, and several photos of the same person are welcome. No family members to register or memorize.

Kithframe is in development. App-store downloads and paid service are not yet available. This site provides the service’s support information, privacy policy, and terms.

A free-form photo studio.

Add up to 100 numbered references for each portrait. Describe who should appear, how they interact, and the mood or style. Every portrait is AI-generated; likeness can change, and large contact-sheet batches can lose small-face detail. Review and approve every result before saving or sharing.

No reusable family profiles.

Photos stay with their draft and portrait history on your device. A new portrait starts empty. Selected references, scene or sketch, and prompt leave your device only when you confirm that request.
